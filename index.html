What is Black Dragon AI?

An AI video studio running on free, open-source engines — no rented AI, no per-render bill. Black Dragon AI is in BETA — a free tryout. Two ways to make a clip: Text to Video — type what you want to see and it renders a cinematic clip; and Image to Video — start from your own photo and describe how it should move, Sora-style. Both run on our own Wan 2.2 engine. A Black Dragon Films LLC studio.

  1. Create a free account — email + password, you're in instantly.
  2. Generate free — you get a 7-day free trial: unlimited free generations for 7 days. Write a prompt — or upload a photo and tell it how to move — pick the length and shape, hit Generate.
  3. Like it? Keep creating — pick a monthly plan or a credit pack, pay with Cash App / Venmo / Chime, credits land on your account.
